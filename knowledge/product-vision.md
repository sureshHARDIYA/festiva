# Product vision

Festiva is a proposed framework-agnostic, lightweight TypeScript engine for contextual festival and weather animations. The initial audience is websites in Norway, with internationally extensible presets.

## Principles
1. Enhance the host website rather than replace its identity.
2. Zero runtime dependencies in the core wherever possible.
3. Prefer polished choreography, articulated movement and environmental interaction over flat image translation.
4. Respect website accessibility, performance, layout and brand standards.
5. Opt-in sound; no unexpected auto-playing audio.
6. Calendar-aware activation, with user-controlled schedule and timezone.
7. Composable packs for Christmas, Halloween, New Year and later Easter, Norwegian Constitution Day, summer, winter and rainy days.

## Constraints and non-goals
Visual direction is not yet accepted. Festiva will not ship a default full-screen cartoon scene or dramatically modify branding. API, licensing, packaging and product name on npm require later decisions. No development or publishing is authorized at this stage.

## Success measures (proposed)
Host-logo anchoring, perceived visual quality, responsive/mobile behavior, no blocked clicks, deterministic cleanup, and graceful reduced-motion behavior.
