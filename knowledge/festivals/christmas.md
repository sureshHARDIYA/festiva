# Christmas / Jul — concept

## Primary direction (pending design approval)
Logo-aware, premium seasonal overlay. The host site's own logo stays intact. Optional small Santa hat settling on its corner, tasteful light strands, single hanging ornament with damped swinging and snow accumulation.

## Motion quality
Use carefully timed easing and physical settling, local anchoring through DOM geometry, responsive adaptations, subtle lighting and synchronized optional sound. Avoid creating an independent cartoon scene.

## Alternative motion study
Articulated sleigh and reindeer in a cinematic path may be prototyped separately, but was *not* approved as a default website effect.

## Scheduling
Norwegian December seasonal window customizable by user. No automatic audio.

## Exit criteria
Prototypes on real website branding, light/dark contrast review, mobile occlusion checks and explicit user approval.
