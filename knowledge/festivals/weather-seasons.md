# Weather and seasonal effects — concepts

- Summer: restrained warm light, butterflies, subtle foliage and environmental motion; avoid intrusive scenic overlays.
- Winter: occasional snowfall, gentle accumulation on eligible elements, winter-light details.
- Rainy days: wind-dependent angled raindrops, splash ripples, cloud shadow and soft environmental ambience.

Weather-driven automatic switching is a possible future optional integration, not a core dependency or confirmed capability. Seasonal modes must be manually configurable, reversible, performance-controlled and accessible. No fixed visual style approved.
