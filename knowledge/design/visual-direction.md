# Visual direction — OPEN, not approved

The previous full-screen scenic GIFs and heavily decorated Festiva wordmark were considered **too artificial** for the desired application. Architecture is promising; visual quality is not yet accepted.

## Current hypothesis
Decorate the website's *existing* logo/header through an overlay positioned relative to live element bounds. Small, high-quality seasonal details should appear to belong to the site, not look like a separate cartoon scene.

## Christmas candidates
- Santa hat that lands and settles on a corner of the logo.
- Warm light string that gently resolves onto the logo outline, with restrained twinkles.
- Single hanging ornament with damped swing.
- Small accumulation of snow with believable settling and very low-density snowfall.

## Design constraints
- Do not recolor/recreate the brand logo by default.
- Avoid all effects at once; use themed variants and intensity controls.
- Preserve contrast and readability; never overlap navigation or actionable controls.
- Work on light/dark backgrounds and responsive layouts.
- Prefer original vector assets with consistent polish, texture and lighting.

## Evaluation before coding
Make 2–3 design prototypes *on real page screenshots or the actual user's logo*. Validate realism, placement, occlusion, motion timing, mobile fit and visual restraint. Get explicit sign-off before implementation.
